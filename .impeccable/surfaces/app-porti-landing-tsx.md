# Landing (signed-out home)

Scope: signed-out `/` of the Porti app. Mode: Persuade. Audience: NEAR Hackathon judges first. Action: sign in to open Porti, or take the no-sign-in Walkthrough; secondary: dial the Phone pocket simulator.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is split by one hard mint line, the signature line. Porti's AI side holds everything she can do (research, track, prepare reviews); your side holds what only your wallet can do (sign, withdraw, change limits). Refuses the category default of hero + three feature cards + logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Porti's incumbent world. Mint #00ec97 line and accents, ink #0c1510, white, deep green #19271f; Fredoka display, Nunito body; cartoon ink outlines with offset shadows; the clipboard-holding companion art.

STORY: Visitors understand that the AI cannot move funds, believe it because of a refusal log from a real chain, see the capped Porti Pocket sitting on the line, read an honest verified/not-yet ledger, then open Porti or the Walkthrough.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The headline "An AI that can't sign." sits on the left, with the lede and the primary "Open Porti" and secondary "Take the walkthrough" actions beside it on the right. Below them, inside the first viewport, a two-column split with a full-height mint line in the centre. Left: "Porti can", the review card that slides in and stops against the line, the verbs and the companion art. Right: a dark wallet side with your verbs and an approve panel. (Side-by-side hero adopted so the split stays in the first viewport.)

FORM: "The line", position 6 on the ordered list; seed key 4fd80ec4.
